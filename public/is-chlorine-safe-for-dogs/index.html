Is Pool Water Safe for Dogs? Chlorine and Salt Pools

What chlorine and salt water pools do to your dog's skin, coat and stomach, and the simple habits that prevent problems.

2 min read · Updated November 2026

Chlorine pools

A well-maintained chlorine pool is generally safe for dogs to swim in. Pool chlorine levels are low, and an occasional swim won't harm most dogs.

That said, chlorine can:

  • Dry and irritate skin and coat, especially with frequent swimming
  • Irritate the eyes, causing redness or squinting
  • Upset the stomach if your dog drinks a lot of pool water

The real danger is the chemicals themselves. Chlorine tablets, liquid chlorine and shock are highly concentrated and can cause burns to the mouth and stomach. Store them locked away, and never let your dog near a floating chlorine dispenser.

Salt water pools

Salt water pools use a generator to make chlorine from salt. The water is far less salty than the ocean, but it still contains a fair amount of salt.

  • Swimming is generally fine.
  • Don't let your dog use the pool as a water bowl. Drinking a lot of it can cause stomach upset, and it isn't a good idea for dogs with heart or kidney disease or on a low-sodium diet.

Simple habits

  1. Provide fresh drinking water by the pool so your dog isn't drinking pool water.
  2. Rinse your dog with fresh water after every swim to remove chlorine or salt.
  3. Dry ears thoroughly. See preventing ear infections.
  4. Moisturize skin if needed with a dog conditioner or a vet-recommended product.
  5. Keep pool chemistry balanced. Test regularly. Very high chlorine or off pH is harder on skin and eyes for everyone.
  6. Brush out loose hair before swimming and clean the skimmer. Dog hair clogs filters quickly.

When to call the vet

Red, persistently squinting eyes, skin that's very red, flaky or broken, or repeated vomiting after swimming should be checked by your vet.

This guide is general information, not veterinary advice. If your dog is sick, hurt or acting unusually, call your veterinarian.

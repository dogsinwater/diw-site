Before you go

Five checks before your dog goes in

Run through these every time, whether it's the beach, the lake or the backyard pool.

See the full checklist
  1. Is the water clear of algae scum and advisories?
  2. Life jacket on for boats, open water and beginners?
  3. Fresh drinking water and shade packed?
  4. Is there an easy, gradual way out of the water?
  5. Short sessions with rest breaks planned?

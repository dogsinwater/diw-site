About Dogs In Water

Some dogs launch themselves off the dock without a second thought. Others won't put a paw in a puddle. Most are somewhere in between, and almost all of them are happier with their people on a beach, a lake or a boat.

Dogs In Water is a practical guide to sharing that with your dog safely. We cover teaching a dog to swim, water safety, beaches, lakes and rivers, boats and paddleboards, pools and the ear, skin and coat care that keeps water fun.

How we write

Water is where dogs have some of their best days and some of their worst emergencies. Our guides are based on veterinary and safety sources, and they're honest about risks like drowning, heatstroke, salt-water poisoning and toxic algae. They're general information, not a substitute for your veterinarian.

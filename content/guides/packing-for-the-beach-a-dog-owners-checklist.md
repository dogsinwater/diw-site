- [ ] **Jug of fresh water or a portable shower** to rinse off salt and sand. [Portable pet showers](amzn:portable+dog+shower+rinser)

- [ ] **Ear-drying solution** if your vet recommends one

## Beach snacks

Keep snacks simple and familiar to avoid upset stomachs. Good options: your dog's regular treats, small pieces of carrot or cucumber, frozen blueberries, or a frozen lick mat for shade breaks. Avoid grapes and raisins, onions, garlic, chocolate, xylitol (in sugar-free snacks) and greasy picnic food. Don't let your dog eat cooked bones from the grill.

Feed a full meal at least an hour before heavy swimming or running, especially for large, deep-chested breeds at risk of bloat.

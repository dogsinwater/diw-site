A beach day is one of the best days a dog can have: digging, running, swimming and sniffing everything. A little planning keeps it that way.

## Find a beach where dogs are welcome

Rules vary by beach, season and even time of day. Many beaches allow dogs only in certain areas or outside summer hours, and many require leashes. Check the city, county or park website before you go, and look for signs when you arrive. Fines are common, and following the rules keeps dog beaches open.

## Timing

- **Early morning or late afternoon** in warm months. Midday sand and sun are hard on dogs.
- **Check the tide.** Low tide gives more room to run, but watch out for stranded jellyfish and sharp shells.
- **Check the surf and water conditions** for rip currents, red tide and bacteria advisories.

## Hazards to watch for

- **Hot sand.** It can burn paws. Test with your hand, and bring booties or stick to wet sand. [Dog booties](amzn:dog+booties+hot+pavement+sand)
- **Drinking sea water.** A little causes mild upset. Too much can cause **salt poisoning**. Offer fresh water every 15–20 minutes.
- **Swallowing sand.** Dogs who dig obsessively or fetch sandy balls can swallow enough sand to block their intestines. Rinse toys and take breaks from digging. Signs include vomiting, a swollen belly and straining.
- **Dead fish, crabs, seaweed and jellyfish.** Eating them can cause vomiting, and some are toxic. Keep your dog close and use "leave it."
- **Fishing hooks and line** near piers and jetties.
- **Waves and rip currents.** Keep swims close to shore. See [moving water safety](/ensuring-your-dogs-safety-in-moving-water/).
- **Sunburn** on pink noses, thin coats and bellies. Use a sunscreen made for dogs. Human sunscreens with zinc oxide are toxic if licked. [Dog sunscreen](amzn:dog+sunscreen)
- **Heatstroke.** See [staying cool at the beach](/protecting-your-dog-from-heatstroke-at-the-beach/).

## Fun beach activities

- Fetch in the shallows with a floating toy
- Digging contests (with breaks, and rinse the face)
- Long walks along the waterline
- [Water games](/10-water-games-your-dog-will-love/) like tag and treasure hunts

## When to go home

Leave before your dog is exhausted. Signs it's time: heavy panting that doesn't settle in the shade, lying down and not wanting to get up, stumbling or ignoring water. Then follow [after-ocean care](/caring-for-your-dog-after-swimming-in-the-ocean/).

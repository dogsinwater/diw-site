A wet dog is slippery, heavy and tired. Hauling them over the side of a boat by the collar is hard on their neck and your back, and in an emergency it may not be possible at all. A ramp or ladder solves that.

## Types

- **Floating ramps** attach to the swim platform or side of the boat and float at the surface, giving your dog a gentle slope to swim up and climb. They're the easiest for most dogs to learn. [Floating dog ramps](amzn:floating+dog+ramp+boat)
- **Dog boat ladders** hook over the side or swim platform with wide, flat, non-slip steps. They take up less room but need more confidence. [Dog boat ladders](amzn:dog+boat+ladder)
- **Folding ramps for the dock** help dogs board from a dock or sea wall. [Folding ramps](amzn:dog+ramp+folding+dock)

## What to look for

- **Rated for your dog's weight,** with room to spare
- **Wide, textured, non-slip surface**
- **Secure attachment** that won't shift as your dog climbs
- **Easy to see from the water:** bright colors help
- **Stores flat** for your boat's space

## Teaching your dog to use it

1. **Start on land.** Set the ramp up at a slight angle and lure your dog up with treats. Reward every step.
2. **Move to the dock**, with the ramp attached and the boat tied up and still.
3. **Practice from the water** in calm conditions, guiding your dog to the ramp and helping them up by the life jacket handle at first.
4. **Repeat until it's automatic.** You want your dog to head for the ramp on their own when they're tired.

Teach this before you need it. In a real emergency, a dog who knows where the ramp is can save themselves.

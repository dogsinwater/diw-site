Most dogs who drown in backyard pools could swim. What they couldn't do was get out. Pool walls are smooth and vertical, and a dog who falls in may paddle along the edge until exhausted.

## Teach the exit

This is the single most important pool safety step.

1. **Start in the water with your dog,** with a life jacket on them.
2. **Guide them to the steps** from different spots in the pool, again and again, with treats on the steps.
3. **Practice until your dog heads to the steps on their own,** from anywhere in the pool.
4. **Refresh the skill each season.**

If your pool has no steps, or your dog can't use them, add a **pool ramp for dogs**. [Pool ramps](amzn:dog+pool+ramp+exit)

## Secure the pool

- **Fence it.** A self-closing, self-latching gate keeps dogs (and kids) out when no one's watching.
- **Consider a pool alarm** that sounds when something enters the water. [Pool alarms](amzn:pool+alarm+pet+motion)
- **Be careful with covers.** Dogs can fall onto soft or loose covers and become trapped under them. Only rigid safety covers rated to hold weight are safe, and a pool should never be left with a partly loose cover.
- **Remove floating toys** when you're done, so your dog isn't tempted back in.

## When your dog swims

- **Supervise every time.**
- **Life jackets** for beginners, seniors, small dogs and flat-faced breeds.
- **Limit gulping.** See [water intoxication](/tips-for-treating-swimming-and-boating-injuries-in-dogs/).
- **Take breaks** in the shade with fresh drinking water.
- **Rinse your dog afterwards.** See [is chlorine safe for dogs?](/is-chlorine-safe-for-dogs/)

## Pool chemicals

Store chlorine tablets, shock and other chemicals in a locked shed or high cabinet. They're corrosive and can cause serious burns and poisoning if a dog chews the container.

## Pool parties

At busy pool parties, assign one person to watch the dog, or keep them inside. With so much going on, it's easy for everyone to assume someone else is watching. Ask guests not to feed your dog.

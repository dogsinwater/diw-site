"My dog's a great swimmer" is the most common reason dogs go without a life jacket. But strong swimmers get tired, get cold, get caught in currents and fall off boats. A life jacket buys time.

## Which dogs need one

**Always:**
- On boats, kayaks and paddleboards
- In open water, currents or waves
- Beginners and puppies learning to swim
- Flat-faced breeds (Bulldogs, French Bulldogs, Pugs, Boston Terriers), short-legged breeds (Dachshunds, Corgis, Basset Hounds) and heavy-chested dogs
- Seniors, and dogs with arthritis or health problems
- Small dogs, who tire and chill quickly

**Strongly recommended:** for any dog in deep water, especially when they're likely to swim for a long time.

## What to look for

- **A grab handle on the back.** This is how you'll lift your dog out of the water or back into a boat. It should be sturdy and stitched into the body of the jacket. [Life jackets with handles](amzn:dog+life+jacket+rescue+handle)
- **Flotation under the chest and neck.** A float panel under the chin helps keep the head up, especially for flat-faced dogs.
- **Bright color and reflective trim** so you can spot your dog in the water.
- **Adjustable straps with buckles** that won't slip when wet.
- **A D-ring for a leash.**

## Getting the fit right

1. **Measure your dog's chest** at the widest point, just behind the front legs, and check the brand's size chart. Weight alone isn't enough.
2. **Snug, not tight.** You should fit two fingers under each strap.
3. **Test it.** Lift your dog gently by the handle. The jacket shouldn't slide forward over the head or let the dog slip out.
4. **Check range of movement.** Your dog should be able to walk, sit and paddle normally.

## Introduce it on dry land

Put the jacket on at home for a few minutes at a time with treats and praise. Then try shallow water, holding the handle while your dog gets used to floating. Most dogs swim more calmly and level in a jacket once they feel how it supports them.

## Care

Rinse the jacket with fresh water after each use and let it dry fully out of direct sun. Check buckles, straps and stitching every season, and replace a jacket that's torn, waterlogged or outgrown.

A life jacket is support, not supervision. Keep watching your dog the whole time they're in the water.
